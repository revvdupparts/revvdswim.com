Questions.

How long do the decals last?

Our vinyl is rated for outdoor use and holds up to sun, rain and car washes for years.

How do I apply a decal?

Clean the surface, peel the backing, press it on with the transfer tape, squeegee out any bubbles, then slowly peel the tape off at a sharp angle.

How do the hoodies fit?

True to size with a relaxed fit. Size up for an oversized look.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship in 2 to 5 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email.

Something wrong with my order?

Email revvdupparts@gmail.com with your order number and a photo of the issue, and we'll make it right.

How do I contact you?

Email revvdupparts@gmail.com or DM us on Instagram at @revvd_swim. We usually reply within a day.
